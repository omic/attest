Discover implementation locations and service endpoints from the user or approved
local configuration. Private product repositories, tenant data and connectors are
not bundled with this skill. The catalog is a readiness guide, not a marketplace
installer or a claim that all these services are available.

For a connected implementation, read its current policy and tools before selecting
the first artifact. An email-generation or staging lock remains in force even when
an offline synthetic reference demonstrates drafts. A prepared artifact, a human
approval and independently verified completion are separate states.
